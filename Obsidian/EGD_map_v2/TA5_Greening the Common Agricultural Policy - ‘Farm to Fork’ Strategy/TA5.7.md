## Tackle climate change: climate change adaptation and mitigation. Ensure that agriculture, fisheries and aquaculture, and the food value chain contribute to the target of reducing greenhouse gas emissions to at least 55% below 1990 levels by 2030 and to the achievement of the objective for a climate neutral Union in 2050.
