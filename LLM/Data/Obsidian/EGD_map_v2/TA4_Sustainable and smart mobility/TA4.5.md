## Starting from 2025, at least 2% of aviation fuels will be green, with this share increasing every five years: 6% in 2030, 20% in 2035, 34% in 2040, 42% in 2045 and 70% in 2050. Hydrogen and fuel produced from cooking oil or waste gases considered green (link agreement Council - Parliament on 25.04.2023)
