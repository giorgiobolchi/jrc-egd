# TA6.39

(urban ecosystems) Member States shall ensure that there is no net loss in the total national area of urban green
space, and of urban tree canopy cover in urban ecosystem areas. Member States may exclude from that total national area the urban ecosystem areas in which the share of urban green space in the urban centres and urban clusters exceeds 45 % and the share of urban tree canopy cover therein exceeds 10 %
