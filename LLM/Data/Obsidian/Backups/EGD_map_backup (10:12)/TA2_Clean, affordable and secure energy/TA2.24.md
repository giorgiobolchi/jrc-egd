#

Each Member State shall ensure that at least 3 % of the total floor area of heated and/or cooled buildings (of buildings which have a total useful floor area of over 250 m2) that are owned by public bodies is renovated each year to be transformed into at least nearly zero-energy buildings or zero-emission buildings in accordance with Article 9 of Directive 2010/31/EU
