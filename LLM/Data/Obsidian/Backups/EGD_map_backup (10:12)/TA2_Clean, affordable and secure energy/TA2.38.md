The strategy sets targets for an installed capacity of at least 60 GW of offshore wind by 2030 and at least 300 GW by 2050. Grounding on the revised TEN-E regulation, EU countries have agreed on new, ambitious long-term goals for the deployment of offshore renewable energy up to 2050 in each of the EU’s five sea basins. As a result, the cumulative EU offshore goals have the following ranges: 109-112 GW by 2030, 215-248 GW by 2040, and 281-354 GW by 2050.
